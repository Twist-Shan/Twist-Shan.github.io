现代教堂的神圣感是如何塑造的How Is the Sense of the Sacred Shaped in Modern Churches?

—— 巴格斯韦德教堂与管风琴教堂的空间异同— Spatial Similarities and Differences between Bagsværd Church and Grundtvig’s Church

November 11, 2026 · 25 min read

摘要：哥本哈根有两座现代的教堂吸引着人们的目光，本文以它们为例，分析现代设计的教堂如何营造宗教的神圣感。我们将着重分析两座教堂中光与材料表面的配合、顶部与构件的空间秩序、面向宗教礼拜仪式的组织，以及这些如何改变人对光影、重量、尺度和自身的感知。进一步，本文将这些内容理解为神圣感得以形成的建筑条件，并讨论它们如何在宗教活动与文化经验中产生意义。Abstract: Two modern churches in Copenhagen attract people's attention. Taking them as examples, this essay examines how churches of modern design create a religious sense of the sacred. It focuses on the interaction between light and material surfaces, the spatial order of ceilings and architectural elements, the organization of religious worship, and how these change people's perceptions of light and shadow, weight, scale, and themselves. It further understands these as architectural conditions for the emergence of sacredness and discusses how they acquire meaning through religious activities and cultural experience.

引言：神圣感如何成为一个建筑问题Introduction: How the Sense of the Sacred Becomes an Architectural Question

在人们对欧洲宗教建筑的印象中，相较于法国、意大利，丹麦的古老教堂并不占据显著的位置。反而是两座建于20世纪的教堂——巴格斯韦德教堂与管风琴教堂——以鲜明而不同的空间表达吸引着游客的注意。尽管采用了不同于传统教堂的建筑形式，两座教堂仍然服务于礼拜与宗教生活，并回应着宗教场所对庄重、宁静与精神寄托的要求。相对统一的材料与色彩、克制的装饰和细腻的自然光，使它们呈现出淡雅的北欧气质；而光线与祭坛、器具、座席及上方空间的配合，又将这种淡雅与礼拜活动联系起来，为神圣感的形成提供了条件。由此，我们很自然地得到一个值得讨论的问题：当宗教建筑采用新的形式时，建筑如何通过光、材料与空间的组织，延续其所需的精神表达？In popular impressions of European religious architecture, Denmark's old churches occupy a less prominent position than those of France and Italy. Instead, two churches built in the twentieth century—Bagsværd Church and Grundtvig’s Church—attract visitors with distinct and contrasting spatial expressions. Although their architectural forms differ from those of traditional churches, both continue to serve worship and religious life, responding to the demands of religious places for solemnity, tranquillity, and spiritual solace. Relatively consistent materials and colours, restrained decoration, and delicate natural light give them an understated Nordic character. The coordination of light with the altar, furnishings, seating, and overhead space connects this restraint with worship and provides conditions for a sense of the sacred. This naturally raises a question worth discussing: when religious architecture adopts new forms, how can the organization of light, materials, and space sustain its spiritual expression?

在走进建筑之前，让我们先来定义“神圣感”。考虑到教堂本身需要作为宗教活动场所，本文将“神圣感”定义为一种空间体验：人，在特定场所中，感到自身的精神活动受到了重新组织，并将这种感受区别于日常，进而与礼拜、纪念或超越性的意义联系起来。这一定义能同时保留个人信仰和环境氛围的作用。本文接下来将讨论建筑如何提供“感受神圣”的条件。Before entering the buildings, let us first define a ‘sense of the sacred’. Considering that a church must itself serve as a place for religious activity, this essay defines it as a spatial experience: in a particular place, a person feels that their mental activity has been reorganized, distinguishes this feeling from everyday experience, and connects it with worship, remembrance, or transcendent meaning. This definition preserves the roles of both personal belief and environmental atmosphere. The following discussion considers how architecture provides the conditions for ‘feeling the sacred’.

《玖章造园》里面的栖居伍论章节把尺度与需要、功能及情感相联系，并追问空间造型如何获得诗意。这一思路为本文提供了方法上的启发：比较教堂及“神圣感”时，应当把构件、尺寸与人在其中的活动和感受结合起来。接下来，本文将以建筑照片、平剖面资料、教堂官方介绍及建筑师的设计说明为依据，依次讨论外观、光影投射、顶部空间和礼拜活动四个方面的意义，并分析两幢建筑的异同。The chapter on five discussions of dwelling in Nine Chapters on Garden Making connects scale with needs, function, and emotion, and asks how spatial form acquires poetry. This approach offers methodological inspiration for this essay: a comparison of churches and sacredness should bring together architectural elements and dimensions with people's activities and feelings within them. Drawing on architectural photographs, plans and sections, official church accounts, and the architect's design statement, the following discussion examines exterior appearance, the projection of light and shadow, overhead space, and worship, comparing the two buildings.

教堂门启：从周遭环境到内部空间The Church Door Opens: From the Surroundings to the Interior

教堂的空间体验始于进入之前。它在城市中的位置、与周边环境的关系，以及人进入的路径，建立了人对内部的预期。两座教堂以不同的空间组织方式，将来访者的注意从日常环境引向礼拜空间。The spatial experience of a church begins before entry. Its location in the city, its relationship with its surroundings, and the approach establish expectations of the interior. Through different forms of spatial organization, the two churches direct visitors' attention from the everyday environment toward the space of worship.

巴格斯韦德教堂位于哥本哈根北部近郊 Gladsaxe，一侧邻近交通较繁忙的主街 Bagsværd Hovedgade，另一侧朝向较安静的Taxvej街，入口也设置在后者一侧。草地与散植的桦树构成了周围的近景，场地没有以连续围墙与道路完全隔开。建筑的外部是平直的白色混凝土墙面、灰色铝制屋顶，拥有简洁的轮廓，没有传统教堂的高塔与宏伟立面。哥本哈根官方旅游介绍也以现代工业建筑来形容它带给人的第一印象。Bagsværd Church stands in Gladsaxe, on Copenhagen's northern outskirts. One side lies near the busy main road Bagsværd Hovedgade, while the other faces the quieter Taxvej, where the entrance is also located. Grass and scattered birches form the immediate surroundings, and a continuous wall does not completely separate the site from the roads. The exterior consists of straight white concrete walls and a grey aluminium roof, with a simple outline and none of the tall towers or grand façades of traditional churches. Copenhagen's official tourism account likewise describes its first impression in terms of a modern industrial building.

走入建筑，采光走廊也给出了极简的风格与明确的边界。乌松的设计说明中说，走廊联系着主厅与其他用房，并通过连续的顶部采光获得自然光。图3中的平行墙面、地面和重复梁架共同提示前行方向，透过玻璃屋顶进入的光则使构件的轮廓逐段显现。走廊并没有依靠昏暗来制造肃穆；它通过视野的收束和构件的重复，让注意逐渐集中到建筑内部。Upon entering, the daylight-filled corridors also present a minimalist style and clear boundaries. Utzon's design statement explains that the corridors connect the main hall with the other rooms and receive natural light through continuous overhead glazing. In Figure 3, parallel walls, the floor, and repeated beams together indicate the direction of movement, while light entering through the glass roof reveals the outlines of elements one after another. The corridor does not rely on darkness to create solemnity; by narrowing the view and repeating architectural elements, it gradually concentrates attention on the interior.

巴格斯韦德教堂给人的印象是朴素的，简约的，甚至是“工业”的，这给建筑的主厅做了极大的反差铺垫。图1当中，主厅的顶面是夸张的曲面，和外部较为克制的形象有着鲜明对比。巴格斯韦德的进入过程，由此表现为一种逐步展开的组织方式——先通过外观营造一种质朴感，再借走廊收束视野，最后使主厅的曲面与光线成为观看的重点。Bagsværd Church gives an impression of plainness, simplicity, and even ‘industry’, preparing a strong contrast with its main hall. In Figure 1, the hall's ceiling is an exaggerated curved surface, sharply contrasting with the restrained exterior. Entry at Bagsværd therefore unfolds gradually: the exterior first establishes plainness, the corridor then narrows the view, and finally the curves and light of the main hall become the focus of attention.

管风琴教堂也同样比较偏远，位于哥本哈根西北部的 Bispebjerg，坐落在当地的高地上，周围是与其共同组合的På Bjerget住宅群。住宅的对称布局顺应了从教堂延伸至比斯佩比约墓园（Bispebjerg Kirkegård）的轴线，墓园的阶梯状山墙等形式也与教堂相呼应。Grundtvig’s Church is similarly somewhat out of the way, in Bispebjerg in northwestern Copenhagen. It occupies high ground and is surrounded by the På Bjerget housing ensemble, composed together with the church. The symmetrical arrangement of the housing follows the axis extending from the church to Bispebjerg Cemetery (Bispebjerg Kirkegård), whose stepped gables and other forms also echo the church.

因此，教堂的突出地位同时来自地形、建筑高度与周边环境的配合。较低的住宅提供了可以比较的尺度，高大的西立面则成为远处视线容易辨认的目标。若沿墓园轴线一侧朝教堂观看，前景的路径与两侧环境会进一步强化它的正面形象。从远处看，砖砌立面的竖向线条与逐级升高的轮廓首先显现；走近之后，注意才逐渐转向砖的砌筑、壁面的凹凸以及下方门洞。整体高度与入口尺度之间形成了明显的对照。人面对的是一座具有纪念性的建筑，但实际进入时，仍须通过一个与身体尺度相对应的开口。外部的宏大形象，因而被落实为穿过门洞这一具体动作。The church's prominence therefore comes from the combination of topography, building height, and surroundings. The lower housing provides a comparative scale, while the tall west façade becomes an easily recognizable target from a distance. Viewed toward the church along the cemetery axis, the foreground path and the surroundings on either side further reinforce its frontal image. From afar, the vertical lines and stepped silhouette of the brick façade appear first; closer up, attention gradually shifts to brickwork, recesses and projections, and the doorway below. A clear contrast emerges between overall height and entrance scale. One faces a monumental building, yet actual entry still requires passing through an opening corresponding to the body's scale. The grand exterior image is thus translated into the concrete act of crossing a doorway.

进入门洞与前室后，视野和光线再次发生变化。图1中，柱列、座席和高处窗洞在更开阔的空间中展开。此前在外部感受到的高耸，在这里转化为身体置于内部之后的尺度关系。管风琴教堂的进入过程因而具有较鲜明的“门槛感”。外部先通过高地、轴线与立面建立预期，门洞和前室再使视野短暂收束，随后展开的中殿反差则强化了纵深与高度的感受。After the doorway and vestibule, the view and light change again. In Figure 1, rows of piers, seating, and high windows unfold in a more open space. The soaring height previously perceived outside becomes a relationship of scale experienced by a body inside. Entry at Grundtvig’s therefore has a pronounced sense of a threshold. The high ground, axis, and façade first establish expectations; the doorway and vestibule briefly narrow the view; the subsequent opening of the nave intensifies the perception of depth and height through contrast.

两幢建筑的都让进入的过程超出了单纯的通行。随着移动，注意力从周遭道路、树木与住宅，逐渐转向光线、构件和礼拜空间。日常环境与教堂内部的反差，由这些具体的空间变化建立起来。这段路为人在其中停留、专注并参与宗教活动提供了起点，也为下一节对光与材料的讨论奠定了基础。Both buildings make entry more than mere passage. As people move, their attention gradually shifts from surrounding roads, trees, and housing to light, architectural elements, and the worship space. These concrete spatial changes establish the contrast between everyday surroundings and the church interior. The route provides a starting point for pausing, concentrating, and participating in religious activity, and prepares the following discussion of light and materials.

光影显现：材料表面与空间塑造的轻盈感Light and Shadow Revealed: The Lightness Shaped by Material Surfaces and Space

进入教堂之后，提到宗教与神性，人们逃不开对光影的讨论。光影需要同时考察开口位置、光线接触的表面和人在空间中的位置。同样是自然光，经由顶部、侧面或低处进入，会使不同构件成为视觉重点；光线照到曲面、砖柱或地面，也会形成不同的明暗边界。因此不同的材料和结构会带来不同的“轻盈感”，我们定义为视觉压力大小的感受。Once inside a church, discussion of religion and divinity inevitably turns to light and shadow. These require simultaneous attention to the position of openings, the surfaces light touches, and a person's position in space. The same natural light makes different elements visually prominent depending on whether it enters from above, the side, or below; falling on curved surfaces, brick piers, or the floor, it also produces different boundaries between light and dark. Different materials and structures thus create different senses of ‘lightness’, defined here as the felt degree of visual pressure.

在巴格斯韦德，曲面使光线的变化成为形体。图5中，教堂主厅的光线主要由高处侧窗进入，经白色曲面向下反射。人的视线沿着弯折移动，感受顶部起伏带来的明暗变化。白色表面进一步减少了色彩差异的干扰，观看者因而更容易感受到形状带来的轻盈。At Bagsværd, curved surfaces turn changes in light into form. In Figure 5, light enters the main hall principally through high sidelights and is reflected downward by white curves. The gaze follows their bends, experiencing changes in brightness produced by the undulating ceiling. White surfaces further reduce the interference of colour differences, making the lightness of the form easier to perceive.

我们再把视角拉远，从图6的较高视点看，顶部曲面与下方座席形成了两种不同的秩序。座席、栏杆和地面提供可计数、可接近的尺度，上方则以连续表面展开。两者共同使光影获得空间位置：亮部也并非独立的装饰，它依附于覆盖的弯曲方向，并与下方活动区域相联系。Pulling the viewpoint back, the higher view in Figure 6 reveals two different orders in the curved ceiling and the seating below. Seats, railings, and the floor offer a countable and approachable scale, while the space above unfolds as a continuous surface. Together they give light and shadow spatial positions: bright areas are not independent decoration but follow the direction of the curved cover and relate to the activity area below.

管风琴教堂的光影编排也十分讲究，明暗的差异使人同时得以同时关注整体与细节。图7中，统一的材料色调使柱、墙与拱顶能够被读作连续的整体，座椅和砖柱的明暗对比则提供了细节的可辨认性。光带来了整体与局部的分离，提供了对比的导引，也给予了规矩带来的“轻盈”。The arrangement of light and shadow at Grundtvig’s is also carefully considered: differences in brightness allow attention to both the whole and its details. In Figure 7, the consistent material palette allows piers, walls, and vaults to be read as a continuous whole, while the contrast between chairs and brick piers makes details distinguishable. Light separates whole and part, offers guidance through contrast, and provides the ‘lightness’ arising from order.

图8则转向祭坛后方，光从高窗打入，首先显现较亮的竖向区域，其间暗部的砖砌壁面使窗与窗又保持着清楚的间隔。视线下移，祭坛、地面和两侧墙体把向上的观看重新带回礼仪发生的位置。在这里，我们得以区分采光来源与观看焦点：窗洞提供光线，具体的注意落在被照亮的壁面、祭坛或构件边缘。建筑的表现力由开口与受光表面的共同组织产生。再看图9，斜向视角使砖柱的厚度与前后遮挡更加明显，建筑的纵深因这些差异而更容易被辨认，人的视线从而顺着光到达教堂的核心，清晰的引导由此减小了视觉的压力。Figure 8 turns toward the back of the altar. Light entering through the high windows first reveals brighter vertical areas; the darker brick walls between them keep each window clearly separated. As the gaze moves down, the altar, floor, and flanking walls bring upward looking back to the place where ritual occurs. Here we can distinguish the source of illumination from the focus of attention: windows supply light, while attention falls on illuminated walls, the altar, or the edges of elements. Architectural expression arises from the joint organization of openings and receiving surfaces. In Figure 9, an oblique view makes the thickness and overlapping of brick piers clearer. These differences make depth more legible, guiding the gaze along the light toward the church's core; this clear guidance reduces visual pressure.

相比巴格斯韦德通过连续曲面呈现较缓和的明暗过渡，管风琴教堂更多借助柱列、窗间墙与肋拱之间的分隔形成层次。两者共同表明，“轻盈感”可以来自对视觉重点的重新组织和对材料与色彩的编排，无须以自身轻薄为前提。进而，这种视觉的“轻盈”进一步放松了心理，使人更容易融入神圣的氛围。Where Bagsværd presents gentler transitions of light and shade through continuous curves, Grundtvig’s creates layers more through the separation of piers, walls between windows, and ribbed vaults. Together they show that ‘lightness’ can arise from reorganizing visual emphasis and arranging materials and colours, without requiring the building itself to be thin or light. This visual ‘lightness’ further relaxes the mind, making it easier to enter a sacred atmosphere.

抬头仰望：屋顶、形状与身体尺度的感知Looking Up: Roofs, Shapes, and the Perception of Bodily Scale

在感受光影之外，神圣感还来自人们对身体尺度的感知，而感知主要来自于对周遭环境的观测与对比。Beyond the experience of light and shadow, a sense of the sacred also comes from people's perception of bodily scale, a perception arising mainly through observation of and comparison with the surrounding environment.

从参观者的角度，结构绝不会先以工程知识的模样被理解，而是通过可见的形态和支承关系影响感受。在普通的房间，平顶往往使人较快地确定高度和边界，而巴格斯韦德的曲面覆盖则增大了难度，使人间接忘记去对比自身的尺度。在这里，轻盈与承托同时存在：头顶有明确的覆盖，表面的连续变化又使其难以被理解成一块平直、静止的天花板，从而促使人放下比较，专注放空。For a visitor, structure is never first understood as engineering knowledge; it affects feeling through visible forms and relationships of support. In an ordinary room, a flat ceiling often makes height and boundaries quickly apparent. Bagsværd's curved cover makes that harder, indirectly causing people to forget to compare their own scale. Here lightness and support coexist: there is a clear cover overhead, yet its continuously changing surface resists being understood as a flat, motionless ceiling, encouraging people to relinquish comparison and focus on emptying the mind.

与之相对，管风琴教堂选择了另一条极端的路——通过极度高挑的顶部与宏大的内饰反衬人的渺小，强化教堂的神性与权威性。而参观者只能靠仰望来欣赏重复的拱顶组织。图12中，柱线向上延伸，并在高处与肋拱相接；多个相似单元沿中殿依次展开，使视线能够在近处与远处之间不断比较。夸张的高度因这种连续关系而被感知，行进中的人也会随着位置变化看到构件相互遮挡、展开。教堂官方资料列出其中殿拱顶内高为22米、侧廊拱顶内高为14米，中殿宽度为10米，说明内部确有显著的高度差。By contrast, Grundtvig’s chooses the opposite extreme: exceptionally high ceilings and a grand interior emphasize human smallness, strengthening the church's divinity and authority. Visitors can appreciate the repeated vaulting only by looking up. In Figure 12, the lines of piers extend upward to meet ribs, while similar units unfold along the nave, allowing the gaze to compare near and far repeatedly. Exaggerated height is perceived through this continuity, and moving visitors also see elements obscure and reveal one another. Official church information gives an internal nave-vault height of 22 metres, an aisle-vault height of 14 metres, and a nave width of 10 metres, demonstrating the substantial differences in height within the interior.

除开单一的大尺度或者不可比较的边界，高处空间是否引人注意，还取决于它与眼前构件之间的联系。座席、通道、圣器或栏杆这些接近身体的构件，使上方覆盖能够与日常活动尺度发生对照，使人置身其中却不自知。巴格斯韦德的曲面和管风琴教堂的挑高，由此消去或弱化了人的位置感。空间尺度之于“神圣感”，可以进一步被理解为建筑与身体之间的耦合。参观者以经验去感知建筑的尺度，而建筑的巧思进一步促使了人的感受产生变化。Beyond a single grand scale or boundaries that resist comparison, whether high spaces attract attention also depends on their relationship to nearby elements. Seating, passages, sacred furnishings, and railings close to the body set the overhead cover against the scale of everyday activity, placing people within it without their being conscious of it. Bagsværd's curves and Grundtvig’s height thereby erase or weaken a person's sense of position. The role of spatial scale in sacredness can further be understood as a coupling of architecture and the body. Visitors perceive architectural scale through experience, while architectural ingenuity further changes their feelings.

神性聚集：从空间氛围到宗教体验Sacredness Gathers: From Spatial Atmosphere to Religious Experience

讨论完建筑特征，让我们回归教堂本身的意义，从“看建筑”转换为“身处礼拜空间”，着眼于人的使用与交互，以及前文的特征如何落到宗教体验的感知。Having discussed architectural features, let us return to the meaning of the church itself, shifting from ‘looking at architecture’ to ‘being in a worship space’. The focus is on human use and interaction, and on how the features discussed above become perceptions of religious experience.

图14中，巴格斯韦德的座席、圣器与管风琴使空间具有明确用途，也把光影与身体能够参与的活动联系起来。建筑的安静、秩序与方向性，只有放在这些活动中才能得到更完整的解释。由此，我们可以区分空间氛围与宗教体验。前者包含可观察的明暗、尺度和材料关系，后者还取决于参与者的信仰、记忆及当时发生的活动。同一个人在参观、冥想和参与礼拜时，可能对同一空间形成不同理解。建筑能够为注意的集中和共同活动提供条件，其宗教意义则需要在具体使用中被赋予和维持，达成实现“神圣感”的最后一步。In Figure 14, Bagsværd's seating, sacred furnishings, and organ give the space a clear purpose and connect light and shadow with activities in which the body can participate. The architecture's quietness, order, and direction become more fully intelligible only within these activities. We can therefore distinguish spatial atmosphere from religious experience. The former includes observable relationships of brightness, scale, and materials; the latter also depends on the participant's beliefs, memories, and the activity taking place. The same person may understand the same space differently when visiting, meditating, or attending worship. Architecture can provide conditions for focused attention and communal activity, while its religious meaning must be assigned and maintained through particular uses, completing the final step toward a sense of the sacred.

首先，我们会注意到这两座现代建筑对材料的使用和装潢的设计都较为克制，颇具北欧的清冷感，但这并不等于宗教符号的消失，材料处理的克制依旧可以使少数礼仪设施获得更清楚的位置。相对统一的墙面与座席背景，减少了多个视觉对象相互竞争的情况，祭坛、十字架、洗礼设施及其他圣器便能够通过位置、形状和使用方式被注意。图9中，管风琴教堂的祭坛使用与教堂相同的黄砖砌筑，表明礼仪焦点可以通过与整体材料相连续的方式得到强调，其特殊性不必完全依靠华丽表面。First, both modern buildings use materials and decoration with considerable restraint, conveying a cool Nordic character. This does not mean that religious symbols disappear: restrained material treatment can still give a small number of ritual furnishings clearer positions. Relatively consistent walls and seating reduce competition between visual objects, allowing the altar, cross, baptismal facilities, and other sacred furnishings to attract attention through position, shape, and use. In Figure 9, Grundtvig’s altar is built of the same yellow brick as the church, showing that a ritual focus can be emphasized through material continuity with the whole; its special character need not depend entirely on ornate surfaces.

接下来，我们再来分析光所照亮的圣器以及其与参观者的交互。比较图14与图15，可以看到两类不同尺度的设施如何参与空间组织。巴格斯韦德的圣器把注意引向能够接近、辨认并参与仪式的位置；管风琴教堂西端的管风琴则以较大的尺度占据中殿另一端，与座席及两侧砖柱形成稳定关系，营造出圣洁的氛围。图15所示的西端大管风琴是管风琴教堂的核心，由Esben Klint设计、Marcussen & Søn制造，完成于1965年。音管、木质框架与砖柱在视觉上构建呼应；在礼拜活动中，管风琴还额外提供了听觉层面的体验，教堂的混响与管风琴空灵又沉稳的音色相得益彰，使人进一步放松神经，感受“神圣”。Next, consider the sacred furnishings illuminated by light and their interaction with visitors. Comparing Figures 14 and 15 reveals how facilities at two different scales participate in spatial organization. Bagsværd's furnishings draw attention toward places that can be approached, recognized, and used in ritual; the organ at Grundtvig’s west end occupies the other end of the nave at a larger scale, forming a stable relationship with seating and flanking brick piers and creating a holy atmosphere. The large west-end organ in Figure 15 is the core of Grundtvig’s Church, designed by Esben Klint, built by Marcussen & Søn, and completed in 1965. Pipes, wooden framing, and brick piers echo one another visually. In worship, the organ adds an auditory experience: the church's reverberation complements its ethereal yet steady tone, further relaxing people and allowing them to feel the ‘sacred’.

结语：神圣感的建筑条件Conclusion: Architectural Conditions for the Sense of the Sacred

收回主线，两座教堂共同说明了神圣感可以在现代的建筑形式中发生，其共同条件在于身体、光与活动之间的关系。巴格斯韦德以克制的外观和起伏的曲面引导注意，管风琴教堂以砖柱、肋拱和纵深组织仰望。入口改变参观的预期，光影使材料获得层次，尺度使人重新感受自身，礼拜则为这些经验赋予共同的意义。本文由此把神圣感解释为一个在人与场所相遇时逐渐形成的过程，需要在人如何使用和感受空间的进程中理解。Returning to the main argument, both churches show that sacredness can emerge in modern architectural forms, with common conditions found in the relationships between body, light, and activity. Bagsværd directs attention through a restrained exterior and undulating curves; Grundtvig’s organizes upward looking through brick piers, ribs, and depth. Entry changes expectations, light and shadow articulate material, scale renews bodily awareness, and worship gives these experiences shared meaning. This essay therefore interprets sacredness as a process gradually formed in the encounter between people and place, to be understood through the ways people use and experience space.

从身体出发，人在两座教堂中感受到的轻盈、渺小或安定，都涉及自身与周围世界的交互与参考。尺度与结构使人意识到身体的有限，座席与圣器又给予身体可以停留和参与的位置。有限性与归属感在这里同时发生：人承认自身无法占据空间的全部，也在共同的朝向中找到与他人、与世界相处的位置。Starting from the body, the lightness, smallness, or stability felt in these churches all involve interaction with and reference to the surrounding world. Scale and structure make people aware of bodily limits, while seating and sacred furnishings give the body places to remain and participate. Finitude and belonging occur together: people acknowledge that they cannot occupy the whole space, yet find, in a shared orientation, a position from which to relate to others and the world.

接着再到人与环境的交互，而这恰恰与乌松关于云与礼拜空间的遐想（图16）不谋而合。他将对巴格斯韦德教堂的构想与夏威夷海岸上云层间透下的阳光联系起来。从云下的自然经验到可供聚集的室内空间，建筑把短暂的感受转化为能够反复进入、停留和共享的场所。混凝土提供覆盖，光影和曲面让覆盖呈现变化，人的参与则使这片覆盖具有礼拜的意义。由此看，建筑的精神表达是从最具体的身体经验出发，在材料、自然与共同生活的联系中展开。因此，光之于神圣感的意义，在于它让人重新看见自身所处的关系，进而引发人与环境的互动。建筑为互动提供条件，信仰与仪式的参与进一步促进了宗教体验。We then turn to the interaction between people and their environment, which happens to coincide with Utzon's imagining of clouds and a worship space (Figure 16). He connected his conception of Bagsværd Church with sunlight passing between clouds along the Hawaiian coast. Moving from natural experience beneath clouds to an interior for gathering, architecture transforms a fleeting feeling into a place that can repeatedly be entered, inhabited, and shared. Concrete provides cover; light, shadow, and curves make that cover change; human participation gives it the meaning of worship. Seen this way, architecture's spiritual expression begins with the most concrete bodily experience and unfolds through connections between materials, nature, and communal life. The significance of light for sacredness therefore lies in making people see anew the relationships they inhabit, prompting interaction with the environment. Architecture provides conditions for interaction, while participation in belief and ritual further advances religious experience.

言而总之，两座现代教堂给予本文的启示是：神圣感可以始于日常感知的一次转变——当人愿意停下、仰望，并把注意给予自身之外的世界，有限的空间便可能容纳超出个人日常事务的意义，去承载一个人对于天地万物的哲思。In sum, the lesson these two modern churches offer is that sacredness can begin with a shift in everyday perception: when people are willing to pause, look upward, and attend to a world beyond themselves, a finite space may hold meanings beyond individual daily affairs and carry a person's reflections on all things between heaven and earth.
